Calendar notification creation is implemented (supersedes the older
fixture-only status sections below):
- Existing single/bulk schedule saves create date-specific WorkdayNotification
  records for active same-department colleagues whose schedule is missing when
  coverage is short. Known days off are asked only after working -> off reduces
  coverage. Vacations, working colleagues and the saving employee are excluded.
- Pending/sent questions remain actionable after reading. Direct responses are
  authenticated, validate the actual notice/date and expected own status, and
  save in a serializable transaction. Concurrent/stale responses return 409.
- Fill requests last until the employee marks their own date, even if the minimum
  staffing is already covered. Replacement questions retire when coverage is
  restored. Explicit answers use notification status `answered`; automatic
  retirement uses `resolved`, which may reopen on a later coverage loss. Historic
  `cancelled` rows are conservatively left closed. No schema change is required;
  the existing notification status is an unrestricted string.
- The existing admin coverage warning remains for reductions of a working plan;
  this adds neither a daily chase scheduler nor forced shifts/closure blocks.
- All dates stay in the existing grouped calendar section. The existing push
  dispatch suppresses repeated unread calendar pushes within the same month;
  bell/attention retain individual questions. Stale recipients/dates/coverage
  are rechecked for attention, bell and delivery.
- Older monthly digests had no stored date list. Their remaining relevant dates
  are derived from the author's audited working-to-off changes and unchanged
  current days off, never from guessed message text. Explicit date answers take
  precedence. This may retain older still-unresolved reductions in that month.

## Accepted behaviour

- Calendar cells remain 52px; do not put “Сегодня” inside a cell or enlarge it.
  Today keeps a copper outline; selected date is separate. Below the calendar,
  today's heading reads “Сегодня”.
- The roster has equal-height rows, aligned statuses and small status dots.
  Own-day editing stays in the own row. Compact spacing preserves touch targets.
- One bulk flow fills and revises current/future working days, keeps existing
  working days preselected and explicitly treats other eligible dates as off.
  Vacation/past days are excluded; stale snapshots reject the whole batch.
- A missing employee schedule date is unknown availability, not a day off.
  When any active employee in the department has neither working/off nor an
  active vacation, offer all department shifts and explain the incomplete plan.
  Do not silently assign the long shift based on one working mark.
- With complete schedules, preserve explicit first choice and remaining paired
  shift, solo shift, and the existing explicit solo exception. Do not infer an
  employee's chosen shift solely from arrival time. QR timestamps, lateness
  calculations and checklist definitions are unchanged.
- Colleague planned-time captions likewise do not guess a solo shift from an
  incomplete plan.
